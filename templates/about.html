How We Make Money

Integration Directory is and will always remain free for users. We sustain our operations through:

  • Affiliate partnerships: When you sign up for tools we recommend (like Make.com), we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only partner with tools we've tested and believe in.
  • Display advertising: We run contextual ads via Google AdSense to cover operational costs.
  • Premium content: Advanced guides and courses (coming soon) for power users.

Our commitment to you: Every recommendation on this site is based on real-world testing and genuine belief in the tool's value. Payment never influences our editorial judgment. If we don't believe in a product, we won't recommend it—even if they offer to pay.
